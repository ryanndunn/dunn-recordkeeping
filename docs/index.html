Mileage Log

A personal app for recording miles driven to appointments, on Android and macOS.

What it does

  • Pick where you're going from your saved places or a Google Maps address search.
  • The trip starts from your last stop that day, or from home, and records the driving distance from Google Maps.
  • Your saved places, settings and (soon) a mileage spreadsheet stay in your own Google account and on your own devices.

Who it's for

Mileage Log is a personal project built for one person's record keeping. It is not sold or offered as a service.
