Privacy policy

Mileage Log · Last updated November 15, 2026

Mileage Log is a personal app for recording miles driven to appointments. It has no servers of its own. Your information stays on your devices and in your own Google account. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to anyone else, except the Google services described below.

Google account access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for two limited permissions:

  • Files the app creates (drive.file): the mileage spreadsheet the app creates in your Google Drive. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • The app's own hidden folder (drive.appdata): a small settings file so your phone and computer show the same settings. It holds your Google Maps API key, home address, preferred navigation app, mileage rate, and which spreadsheet to use. This folder is hidden in Google Drive and only Mileage Log can read it.

The app also reads your Google account's email address, only to show which account is signed in.

Mileage Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is used only to provide the app's features to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.

Information stored on your devices

  • Your saved places, logged trips, settings, and remembered driving distances are stored on each device.
  • Your Google Maps API key and your Google sign-in are kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore or the macOS Keychain).

Google Maps services

To find addresses and measure driving distances, the app sends the text you type in the address search, and the start and end points of each trip, to Google Maps Platform (Places API and Routes API) using your own API key. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No tracking of your location in the background.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your information.

Removing your information

  • Stop Google access: sign out in the app's Settings, or remove Mileage Log at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the hidden settings file: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find Mileage Log, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete the spreadsheet: delete it from Google Drive like any other file.
  • Delete on-device data: uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
